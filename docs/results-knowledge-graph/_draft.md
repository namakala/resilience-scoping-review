---
title: "Knowledge Graph Results"
description: "Structural findings from the ontology-guided thematic analysis knowledge graph"
---

The analysis began with 644 exemplars from the scoping review literature. A 31-node hierarchical ontology with four conceptual domains organised these exemplars: problem definition, resilience mechanisms, resolution pathways, and theoretical frameworks. Within each ontology node, conceptually similar exemplars formed 49 codes. Fourteen broader themes aggregated these codes, each drawing from a single ontology domain. Six interpretations then drew from these themes, each spanning a connected region of the ontology hierarchy. This entire structure formed the knowledge graph.

The graph preserved analytical lineage from exemplars through themes and codes to each interpretation. Structural rules prevented cross-domain contamination and disconnected synthesis by requiring themes to group codes from a single ontology domain and interpretations to span connected regions of the hierarchy. The graph also enabled incremental updates by propagating a dirty flag only to downstream entities when the author revised a code or theme. Only ontology branches with pending changes required reprocessing. This avoided full re-analysis on each iteration. Themes grouped a mean of 2.9 codes with a range of 2 to 5. Interpretations synthesised a mean of 2.7 themes with a range of 2 to 4.

Six interpretations emerged from the analysis. Three came from the problem definition domain and addressed the cognitive mechanisms of stress appraisal and the emotional mediation pathways through which stress amplified. These same mechanisms explained patterns of vulnerability during life transitions. Two came from the resilience mechanisms domain and described resilience as a changeable capacity developed through experience and how structured interventions could cultivate and measure this capacity. One came from the resolution pathways domain and described problem-solving, coping, and support strategies as concrete mechanisms for enacting resilience. The theoretical frameworks domain did not produce an independent interpretation. Its 20 exemplars informed codes that were synthesised into interpretations across other ontology domains. The domain provided framing concepts rather than a distinct phenomenon.
